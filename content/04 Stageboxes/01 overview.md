# Stageboxes Overview

This category contains specifications and reference documentation for remote audio stageboxes and digital snake expanders.

## Information Stored Here
- **Preamps & I/O**: Remote-controllable microphone preamplifiers, analog line returns, AES/EBU connections
- **Networking & Connectivity**: AES50, Dante, AES67, MADI, etherCON ports
- **Console Compatibility**: Supported ecosystem brands and console model compatibility
- **Form Factors & Construction**: Stage rackmount (19″), drop boxes, rugged road casing, dual redundant power supplies
