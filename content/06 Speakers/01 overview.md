# Speakers Overview

This category contains specifications and reference documentation for point-source loudspeakers, stage wedges, and fill speakers.

## Information Stored Here
- **Speaker Types**: Point-source front-of-house cabinets, stage floor wedges, coaxial monitors, front fills, column PA
- **Amplification Type**: Active (powered) or passive (unpowered) operation
- **Transducers**: HF compression drivers (rotatable horns) + LF driver sizes (8″, 10″, 12″, 15″)
- **Coverage & Dispersion**: Nominal coverage angles (e.g. 90° × 60°), frequency range, crossover points
- **Mounting & Hardware**: 35mm pole sockets, multi-angle monitor wedge angles, M10 fly points, wall-mount brackets
