The Shure SM7B is a legendary dynamic cardioid microphone renowned for broadcast, podcasting, voiceover, and studio vocal applications.

It delivers a smooth, flat, wide-range frequency response with built-in air suspension shock isolation and internal electromagnetic shielding to defeat hum from computer monitors and studio equipment.

## Key Features
- **Flat, wide-range response**: Natural reproduction for voice and musical instruments.
- **Switchable EQ controls**: Bass roll-off and mid-range emphasis (presence boost) with graphic display of settings.
- **Rejection of electromagnetic hum**: Optimized shielding against broadband interference.
- **Internal air suspension shock isolation**: Virtually eliminates mechanical noise transmission.
- **Integrated swivel mount**: Captive stand nut for easy mounting and precise microphone positioning.

## Typical use
- Studio vocal tracking
- Podcasting and broadcast radio
- Voiceover narration
- Close-miking brass, guitar cabinets, and snare drums
