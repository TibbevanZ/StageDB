# Microphones Overview

This category contains specifications and reference documentation for microphones.

## Information Stored Here
- **Microphone Type & Transducer**: Dynamic, condenser, ribbon, boundary, wireless capsules
- **Polar Patterns**: Cardioid, supercardioid, omnidirectional, figure-8, multi-pattern
- **Acoustic & Electrical Specs**: Frequency response ranges, impedance, sensitivity, max SPL, self-noise
- **Physical & Connectivity**: Connector types (XLR, mini-XLR, TA4F), form factors, phantom power requirements
- **Typical Applications**: Live vocal, instrument miking, studio recording, broadcast, speech
